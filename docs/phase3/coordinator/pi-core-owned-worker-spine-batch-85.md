nominated pins and originals in the read-only evidence stores; do not take
expectations from the submitted report. The reader checks the reviewed
wrapper/entry in the nominated host profile, the restricted launch profile,

worker's retained mount table. The existing replay still checks stopped task,
root, cwd, descriptors, mask evidence and state-device exclusions.

as evidence of Pi's socket permissions or task-relative links. The B83 gate

installed image or previously retained evidence was changed.
